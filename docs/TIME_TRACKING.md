## Instructions

- Record time in hours (e.g., `1.5 h`) or minutes (`45 m`) consistently within a row.
- Calculate **Difference** as `Actual - Planned` (use `+` when over estimate, `-` when under estimate).
- Always fill in **Explanation of Differences** when `Difference` is non-zero (e.g., unexpected Phaser issue, extra testing, scope change).
- Do not estimate retroactively without noting that the value is approximate.
